Where are consumer complaint responses running late?

Explored 62,516 consumer complaint records to compare response timeliness by product and identify issue clusters for further review.

↗

Two different signals. Checking and savings had the most untimely responses (867); debt collection had the highest untimely rate (6.44%).

A late response does not mean a complaint was unresolved. These patterns suggest where to investigate; they do not establish root causes.

What skills show up in the data analyst job market?

Queried 2023 remote job postings to compare skill demand, salary patterns, and the tools appearing in higher-paying roles.

↗

SQL led the field. It appeared in 3,083 postings and in 8 of the 10 highest-paying roles reviewed.

The data represents 2023 postings. A skill’s average salary is an association, not evidence that the skill itself causes higher pay.
